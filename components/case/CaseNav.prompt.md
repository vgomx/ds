CaseNav — always the last thing on the page.

```jsx
<CaseNav prev={{ title: 'Internet brand facelift', href: '/work/facelift' }} next={{ title: 'Video platform identity', href: '/work/video' }} />
```

- Hover moves the text from secondary to ink and slides the arrow 4px outward.
- On the first case, omit `prev`; Next stays on the right. On the last case, point Next at the first.
